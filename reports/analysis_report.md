# Analysis summary

- Posts analysed: 2,500  |  comments: 19,829  |  unusual posts: 73
- Dataset contains SIMULATED records: findings describe the simulation, not real audiences.

## 1. Content (median engagement index, 1.0 = typical)

- Reel: 2.10 (95% CI 1.93-2.28, n=485)
- Styling Video: 1.49 (95% CI 1.36-1.63, n=297)
- Influencer Post: 1.31 (95% CI 1.18-1.50, n=262)
- Eid Campaign: 1.10 (95% CI 1.01-1.22, n=381)
- Sale Announcement: 0.60 (95% CI 0.56-0.65, n=710)
- Product Photo: 0.46 (95% CI 0.42-0.50, n=365)
- Kruskal-Wallis p = 5.25e-203

## 2. What the audience wants

- Praise / brand love: 23.0% of comments (negative 2%) e.g. MashaAllah bohat acha lag raha hai 🙏
- Discount seekers: 14.4% of comments (negative 5%) e.g. Bohat khoobsurat hai! Discount milega is suit pe? 🙏
- Availability / stock questions: 11.4% of comments (negative 6%) e.g. COD available hai?
- Price questions: 9.8% of comments (negative 9%) e.g. is dress ki qeemat kya h?
- Ready-to-buy (purchase intent): 7.5% of comments (negative 1%) e.g. Mujhe ye order karna hai, kaise karun?
- Price objections: 6.2% of comments (negative 88%) e.g. TOO EXPENSIVE FOR A LAWN KURTA.
- Fabric-quality questions: 6.1% of comments (negative 16%) e.g. kapda patla toh nahi hai?
- Delivery complaints: 5.8% of comments (negative 94%) e.g. Itna intezar kar liya order ka
- Sizing questions: 5.5% of comments (negative 6%) e.g. کیا ایکس ایل سائز مل جائے گا؟
- Delivery questions: 5.2% of comments (negative 5%) e.g. کیا فیصل آباد میں ڈیلیوری ہوتی ہے؟
- Unclear / needs review: 5.1% of comments (negative 5%) e.g. How much for this lawn suit?

## 5. Recommended actions

- **High / Content**: Shift creative effort and paid reach towards Reel and away from Product Photo.  
  _Evidence: Reel: median engagement index 2.10 (95% CI 1.93-2.28, 485 posts) vs Product Photo: 0.46 (0.42-0.50, 365 posts). Kruskal-Wallis p=5.25e-203._
- **High / Promotion**: Review pricing and value messaging for Brand D (fabric details, bundles, instalments) - price pushback is concentrated there.  
  _Evidence: 'Price objections' make up 10% of comments on Brand D (brand) vs 6% overall = 1.6x (4049 comments)._
- **High / Customer service**: Escalate delivery performance for Brand C: audit courier SLAs, send proactive tracking updates and assign a person to answer order-status comments.  
  _Evidence: Delivery complaints are 11% of comments for Brand C (brand) vs 6% overall = 1.9x (4067 comments)._
- **High / Sales**: Treat Influencer Post comments as hot leads: add a clear 'order via WhatsApp/inbox' call-to-action and reply within the hour.  
  _Evidence: Purchase-intent comments are 13% of comments on Influencer Post vs 7% overall = 1.7x (2058 comments)._
- **High / Customer service**: Investigate immediately: 23 post(s) are dominated by complaints. Reply publicly and fix the underlying issue.  
  _Evidence: Worst: P0458 (Brand E, Reel), P2478 (Brand D, Sale Announcement), P2461 (Brand B, Influencer Post)._
- **Medium / Content**: Prioritise Reel on TikTok - the strongest platform/format pairing.  
  _Evidence: Median engagement index 2.52 across 136 posts (overall typical = 1.0)._
- **Medium / Promotion**: Put the discount, sale price and promo code directly in the caption (and pin a comment) for Sale Announcement posts.  
  _Evidence: 'Discount seekers' make up 27% of comments on Sale Announcement (content type) vs 14% overall = 1.9x (5740 comments)._
- **Medium / Promotion**: Publish clear offer terms and end dates at the start of the 12.12 Sale campaign to pre-empt discount questions.  
  _Evidence: 'Discount seekers' make up 21% of comments on 12.12 Sale (campaign) vs 14% overall = 1.5x (4105 comments)._
- **Medium / Customer service**: Add delivery times, charges and COD availability to Eid Campaign posts / pinned FAQ.  
  _Evidence: Delivery questions are 7% of comments on Eid Campaign vs 5% overall = 1.3x._
- **Medium / Customer service**: Publish city-wise stock/delivery information and consider local stockists for Multan, Lahore, Hyderabad.  
  _Evidence: 980 availability/delivery questions name a city; most frequent: Multan (122), Lahore (112), Hyderabad (106)._
- **Medium / Campaign testing**: A/B test a 'link in bio / WhatsApp order' CTA on Influencer Post content vs the current caption; success metric = inbox enquiries per 1,000 views.  
  _Evidence: Derived from the purchase-intent concentration above._
- **Medium / Content**: Study and replicate 11 over-performing post(s): reuse the format, hook and timing, and boost them with paid reach.  
  _Evidence: Top: P1955 (Brand B, Product Photo), P0730 (Brand A, Sale Announcement), P2107 (Brand D, Eid Campaign)._
- **Medium / Content**: Audit 17 post(s) whose likes are not matched by comments (possible paid/bot likes) before using them as benchmarks.  
  _Evidence: Top: P1853 (Brand E, Sale Announcement), P1995 (Brand A, Product Photo), P1594 (Brand A, Sale Announcement)._
- **Low / Content**: Mention stock status/size availability on Eid Campaign posts and use 'few pieces left' or restock notices.  
  _Evidence: Availability questions are 17% of comments on Eid Campaign vs 11% overall = 1.5x._
- **Low / Content**: Review 10 under-performing post(s) for creative, timing or targeting problems.  
  _Evidence: Lowest: P1349 (Brand E, Product Photo), P0841 (Brand A, Styling Video), P1732 (Brand E, Sale Announcement)._
